PunchLedger, the kiosk in your browser

An offline-first Android time clock for small businesses. This page replays the real flows: PIN kiosk, punches, weekly math, manager area, and recovery. Everything runs in this tab and nothing is saved. The Android app keeps its records in SQLite on the device.

Take the tour

  1. Enter 1111 to sign in as Ava, then clock in. The confirmation returns to the PIN screen after 5 seconds.
  2. Sign in as Marcus (2222). He is already clocked in, and the earlier weeks hold an overnight shift that splits across the Monday week boundary.
  3. Press Simulate double-tap below right after a punch and watch the transaction get rejected in the log.
  4. Enter a wrong PIN five times to see the 30 second lockout.
  5. Switch to manager sign-in with 2468, then try Team and Export.
  6. Choose Forgot manager PIN? and use the recovery code from the table.
